## Required job-description fields

1. Role title and version
2. Role purpose and measurable outcomes
3. Responsibilities and recurring deliverables
4. Decision rights and spending/approval limits
5. Reporting line and key interfaces
6. Required skills and evidence of proficiency
7. Performance indicators with formulas and data sources
8. Work conditions and required tools
9. Review date, author, and approver

## Quality rules

* Describe outcomes and observable responsibilities, not personality traits.
* Assign one accountable owner per result wherever practical.
* Define KPI formula, unit, reporting frequency, source, and exclusions.
* Keep role requirements separate from candidate-specific evaluation.
