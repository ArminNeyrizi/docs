# Professional Etiquette

> **Status: Draft — needs subject-matter review.** The original Markdown file was empty, so this page is a structured course draft rather than a recovered original.

## Course purpose

Help learners practice professional communication and reliable conduct in academic, project, and workplace settings.

## Proposed learning outcomes

By the end of the course, learners should be able to:

* Write clear, respectful work messages with an appropriate level of detail.
* Prepare for meetings, contribute constructively, and record decisions and actions.
* Give and receive feedback without personal attacks or ambiguity.
* Manage commitments, deadlines, confidentiality, and escalation appropriately.
* Communicate risks early and distinguish facts, assumptions, and opinions.

## Proposed modules

1. Written communication: email, chat, task comments, and response expectations.
2. Meetings: preparation, agendas, decision logs, and follow-up actions.
3. Reliability: commitments, deadlines, blockers, and early escalation.
4. Feedback and disagreement: observable behavior, impact, and requested change.
5. Team conduct: respect, inclusion, confidentiality, and conflict of interest.
6. Remote and asynchronous collaboration.

## Assessment draft

* Short scenario-based quiz.
* Rewrite an unclear or unprofessional message.
* Submit a meeting note with decisions, owners, and due dates.
* Role-play a feedback or escalation conversation.
