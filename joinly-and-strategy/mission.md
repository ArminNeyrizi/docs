# Joinly Mission

> **Status: Draft — proposed mission statement, not yet approved.**

## Proposed mission

Keep education connected to the world of work by helping people learn through structured study, practical projects, and evidence of demonstrated ability.

## What this mission implies

* Learning should lead to observable work, not only course completion.
* Projects and assessments should test skills that matter in practice.
* Employers should be able to understand a learner's demonstrated capabilities.
* Learning paths should be updated using evidence from projects and market needs.

## Decision boundaries

This mission describes the intended direction; it does not promise employment, guarantee job outcomes, or claim that every course is already linked to an employer project.
