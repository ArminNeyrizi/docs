## Overview

Joinly is a project-based talent development ecosystem intended to keep education connected to real work. Its operating idea is to combine structured learning with practical projects, assessment, and updates informed by market needs.

## How it works

* **Learn:** structured courses and learning paths.
* **Practice:** projects that require learners to apply concepts to real deliverables.
* **Demonstrate:** assessments and project evidence that make skills easier to evaluate.
* **Connect:** opportunities for companies to discover and work with prepared talent.
* **Improve:** update learning content using project outcomes and employer feedback.

## Intended users

* Learners who want demonstrable, job-relevant skills.
* Instructors and experts who design and assess practical learning.
* Companies that need to evaluate and develop talent.

## Positioning

Working phrase: «نذاره دنیای کار از آموزش فاصله بگیره» — keeping education connected to the world of work.
