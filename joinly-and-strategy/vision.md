# Joinly Vision

> **Status: Draft — proposed future state, not yet approved.**

## Proposed vision

A world where people can move from learning to demonstrable capability and meaningful work through a connected system of education, projects, assessment, and employer collaboration.

## Observable signs of progress

* Learners complete practical work that can be reviewed by others.
* Companies can assess demonstrated skills rather than relying only on credentials.
* Learning paths evolve when project and employer evidence reveals skill gaps.
* Project outcomes, learner outcomes, and economic value are measured separately.

## Assumptions to validate

* Which learner and employer segments should Joinly serve first?
* Is the long-term model primarily a platform, a talent factory, an academy network, or a combination?
* Which outcomes should define success: learning gain, employment, company value, revenue, or a weighted set?
