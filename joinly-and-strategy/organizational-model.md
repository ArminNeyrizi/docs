# Joinly Organizational Model

> **Status: Draft framework — not an approved organization chart.**

## Purpose

Define how strategy, product, learning, projects, sales, marketing, and operations coordinate as Joinly grows.

## Proposed capability areas

| Capability            | Primary accountability                           | Typical outputs                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Leadership & strategy | Priorities, portfolio, resource allocation       | Strategy, quarterly priorities, decision log        |
| Product & engineering | Platform reliability and user workflows          | Shipped product, technical documentation            |
| Learning & quality    | Curriculum, assessment, learning paths           | Courses, rubrics, learning outcomes                 |
| Project delivery      | Practical projects and deliverables              | Project briefs, reviewed work, portfolios           |
| Sales & partnerships  | Company relationships and revenue                | Qualified opportunities, proposals, agreements      |
| Marketing & community | Demand generation and audience engagement        | Campaigns, content, qualified leads                 |
| Data & operations     | Metrics, systems, reporting, process improvement | Dashboards, documented processes, operating reviews |

## Design rules

* Assign one directly accountable owner to each outcome.
* Separate decision authority from execution responsibility.
* Avoid creating a role until a recurring workload or measurable outcome requires it.
* Keep cross-functional handoffs explicit.
